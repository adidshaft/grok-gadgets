# Community guidelines

Discuss gadgets for Grok. Help beginners. Do not ask anyone to share credentials.

When you share a build or ask for help:

1. Search the known issues.
2. Give the software versions and the steps to reproduce the result.
3. Remove credentials and private data from logs and screenshots.
4. State the evidence level: simulated, build verified, Grok verified, hardware verified, or independently reproduced.

Keep technical records in the repository that owns the component. Link to those records in community posts.

Do not post promotional spam. Do not send unsolicited messages.

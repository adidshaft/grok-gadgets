## Help wanted: repeat the kit

We are looking for one person who is not the owner to repeat the ESP32-S3 OLED kit from the
public guide. A failed or partial run helps just as much: tell us the step where the guide
stopped working.

**What changed since the call above:** gadgets join home Wi-Fi from a phone, you claim yours
with the code on its screen, the relay sends signed updates, and the sign-in page now shows
you where to find that code. Current releases: gateway `0.1.0a20`, ESP32 SDK `v0.2.0-alpha.19`, Linux SDK `0.1.0a10`; check the [support matrix](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets/blob/main/docs/public/support-matrix.md) before you start.

**What you do (about two hours):**

1. Follow the [repeatable kit guide](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets/blob/main/docs/getting-started/repeat-the-kit.md).
2. Fill in the [report template](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets/blob/main/community/drafts/phase13/report-template.md) and post it here.
3. Mark each step Pass, Fail or Not tested. Say what you saw on the board, not only what
   the commands replied.

**What you need:** the parts listed above, a USB-C data cable, a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and a
Mac or Linux computer. Windows is not tested. Grok Bot on an iPhone or an Android phone is
welcome but not required.

**What we do:** we answer within a working day and fix the guide where it failed you. Your
report goes into the verification records under your GitHub name only if you agree.

**What this is not:** a product or a service. The project runs no relay for you, and you do
not need a public relay for this test.

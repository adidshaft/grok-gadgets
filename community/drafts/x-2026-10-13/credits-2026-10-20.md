# Phase 14, credits per account on a relay. Draft only (20 Oct 2026), two posts

## 1/2 · attach credits-demo-side-by-side.mp4

grok gadgets can now be shared with others on credits

what it means: whoever runs the relay gives each person a credit balance. 1 credit per command, 1 per day their gadget is online, reading is free

how it works: i shared my esp32 s3 with a friend and gave them 5 credits. they sent texts from their @grok @bot, 5 showed up on the oled, the 6th got "your credits on this relay are used up; ask its operator". their list still worked and my own account is never charged

what it takes: one flag on your relay (--credits) and one command to grant credits. the relay signs a receipt per person every day and a report checks it against the log. a top-up and a restart kept every balance. no payments, no tokens

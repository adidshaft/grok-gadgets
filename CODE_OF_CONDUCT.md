# Community conduct

Treat others respectfully. Explain technical disagreements with evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and nonconsensual account linking are prohibited. Protect household privacy and never request credentials in public reports.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Consequential moderation requires human review and an appeal route. A private reporting route and designated moderation contacts must be established before public launch; until then this is a local preparation document.

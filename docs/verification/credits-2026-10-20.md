## Software tests

Gateway `tests/test_credits.py` and `tests/test_credits_receipts.py` (595 tests pass on `dev`):

- **Bypass:** a command at zero does not reach the gadget. A refused or repeated command costs nothing. A retry of a known command ID gets its answer at zero.
- **Cross-account:** each account sees only its own balance; a command for another account's gadget costs nothing.
- **Double spend:** 40 concurrent charges against 5 credits: exactly 5 succeed.
- **Usage hook:** with every hook line dropped, balances are unchanged.
- **Off and stdio:** without `--credits`, no credits files appear and records are unchanged. stdio and operator mode are never gated.
- **Store:** existing accounts migrate at balance 0 without a change to `accounts.json`. A torn last ledger line is trimmed; a damaged middle line stops the relay.
- **Receipts:** a changed, missing, reordered or other-key receipt fails, and so does one that disagrees with the ledger.

## Limits

- One relay, one kit and two people; the session lasted about 30 minutes.
- Gadget-day metering ran only in software. On the live relay the kit belongs to the operator, who is exempt.
- The daily receipt after midnight UTC is tested with a software clock; the live receipts came from `credits close`.
- No money moved. Credits are a usage measure on this relay.

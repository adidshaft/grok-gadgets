# Claim your gadget — 17 October 2026

Phase 11 ([#268](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets/issues/268), [ADR 011](../decisions/011-accounts-and-claim-codes.md)). Two people, each with Grok Bot on their own iPhone, used one relay and the owner's ESP32-S3 OLED kit. **A** is the owner (the operator account after the migration). **B** is a second person with their own Grok Bot. Times are UTC. The owner authorized the relay upgrade, the migration, the kit flash and each step with B.

Evidence levels:

- **Log**: the relay's request log, read after each step. Account IDs are shortened to A and B; codes never reach the log.
- **Console**: the kit's USB console `status` reply (only while it was on the Mac).
- **Owner**: what A and B saw on their phones and on the kit.

## Result

Pass for every Phase 11 acceptance item: A unclaimed the kit; B added the relay, signed in with the code the kit showed, claimed it and showed text; A could not list, read, command or see events of it; B shared it to A, A used it, B revoked; wrong and expired codes were refused; a flood was limited and logged; after B's BOOT reset and phone re-join, A's claim stayed pending until B unclaimed, and then A showed text. The owner's relay, kit and sign-in kept working through the migration (no new sign-in, no reprovisioning).

Found and fixed during the run (gateway, not released yet):

- [#197](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets-gateway/pull/197): a right code now redirects straight back to Grok Bot; the recovery code comes from `gadgets_share action=recovery_code`.
- [#199](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets-gateway/pull/199): the sign-in page's `form-action` CSP blocked that redirect on phones.
- [#201](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets-gateway/pull/201): a limited code attempt is logged; `service install` right after `service uninstall` no longer reports the device port in use (the relay was down about 100 s and 45 s during the two redeploys).
- Open: during a pending transfer the refusal says "This share lets you read the gadget", which is wrong; the new owner can neither read nor command until the transfer completes.

Not tested: the 7-day grace period on a real relay (unit-tested with a clock); the relay-wide flood limit of 200 codes an hour (unit-tested); Grok Bot on Android; a gadget without a display.
